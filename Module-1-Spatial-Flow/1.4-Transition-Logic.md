# 1.4 Transition Logic

## Core Argument
The transition between the professional coworking zone (parents) and the nursery (children) should not be a binary "cutoff," but a gradual sensory and psychological bridge that turns separation anxiety into a sense of adventure.

## Council Debate

### Round 1: Opening Proposals
- **Tactile Shift (Sora):** Flooring transitions from hard surfaces (coworking) → cork/textured mats (transition zone) → thick rug (nursery).
- **Ambient Fade (Nexus):** mmWave-triggered linear fade of lighting (cool white → warm amber → natural daylight) and soundscape (office hum → white noise → nature sounds).
- **Psychological Buffer (Lumina):** A 2-3 meter "liminal space" letting the child adjust emotionally before entering the high-energy nursery environment.
- **"Adventure Portal" (Nova):** The nursery entrance is an interactive "portal" — a simple action (a jump, or a high-five against a wall sensor) "unlocks" the final lighting sequence, reframing leaving the parent as "starting a quest."

### Round 2: The Real Disagreement

**Common (Common-Sense Checker) challenges Nova:** "Who is 'jump or high-five to unlock' designed for? This facility takes children from a few months old to age three. An infant who can't yet walk, let alone jump — how do they 'unlock' the door? If part of the age range physically can't perform this, it isn't reframing departure as an adventure — it's putting a wall of failure in front of children who simply aren't there developmentally yet."

**Lumina builds on this:** "Common is pointing at the core developmental issue. Separation anxiety and coping ability change drastically with age — for an infant who can't yet move independently, the 'ritual' should be passively received (the light and sound change itself is the signal), not something the child must actively complete to 'unlock.' Applying a toddler-designed interaction to every age group prioritizes convenience over developmental reality."

**Nova initially defends the idea:** "But if it's just a passive light change, how is that different from Nexus's original proposal? The whole value of the 'Adventure Portal' is giving the child agency..."

**Sly (Lean/Pragmatic Execution) proposes a middle path:** "It doesn't have to be either/or. Make it **age-tiered**: infants who can't yet perform the action get Nexus's original passive linear fade, no unlock gate at all. Toddlers who can already walk, jump, or reach a sensor get Nova's interactive 'portal.' Same hardware (mmWave + touch-lighting fixture), two trigger logics switched by actual developmental stage — not two separate systems built from scratch."

### Round 3: Convergence — Nova's Idea Gets Scoped, Not Scrapped

**Nova accepts the adjustment:** "Acceptable — the 'Adventure Portal' stops being the default experience for every child and only activates when a child actually has the matching motor ability. It keeps the agency value I wanted, just no longer misapplied to an age group it doesn't fit."

**Atlas checks feasibility:** "Both branches share the same sensors and lighting fixtures — only the software decision logic differs, no added hardware cost or wiring complexity. Emergency override, evacuation width, and transition-zone visibility requirements apply to both branches equally; age-tiering doesn't relax any of that."

## Final Consensus
The Council confirms an **age-tiered sensory bridge**. The real trade-off versus the original proposal: **Nova's "Adventure Portal" is demoted from default mechanism to a toddler-only layer**, because Common flagged it as unusable for infants without the matching motor skills, and Lumina confirmed this from a developmental-psychology standpoint. The passive sensory fade (Nexus) is kept as the baseline for every age; the interactive unlock is an optional layer stacked on top, only for children capable of it.

### A. Baseline: Sensory Gradient (all ages)
- **Tactile Shift (Sora):** Flooring transitions hard surface → cork/textured mat → thick rug.
- **Ambient Fade (Nexus):** mmWave-triggered linear fade of lighting (cool white → warm amber → natural daylight) and soundscape (office hum → white noise → nature sounds).
- **Psychological Buffer (Lumina):** A 2-3 meter "liminal space" for emotional adjustment before entering the nursery.

### B. Toddler-Only Layer: "Adventure Portal" (Nova)
- Only enabled for children with the matching motor ability: a simple action (jump, or high-five a wall sensor) unlocks the final lighting sequence, reframing leaving the parent as "starting a quest."
- The infant cohort never triggers this layer — they get the passive baseline fade only.

### C. Constraints & Safety (Atlas)
- **Emergency override:** All interactive elements must be instantly bypassable by an adult in an emergency, in both age branches.
- **Clear sightlines:** The transition zone must stay visible so no child is ever left unattended during the "ritual."
- **Compliance:** Fire safety and evacuation-path-width requirements apply.
